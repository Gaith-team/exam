Definitions & pathophysiology
  • DM: metabolic syndrome – hyperglycemia + glycosuria, secondary effect on protein, lipids, electrolytes; due to ↓ insulin secretion or ↓ insulin responsiveness.
  • GDM: diagnosed in the 2nd or 3rd trimester, not clearly overt diabetes before gestation. Cause: can’t keep adequate insulin response because insulin sensitivity drops as gestation advances.
  • No insulin → glucose can’t enter muscle/fat cells → protein catabolism, ketosis, negative nitrogen balance → glucose rises → cellular water lost, glucosuria → extracellular dehydration (osmotic pressure).
  • Pregnancy is diabetogenic: hPL, estrogen, progesterone, cortisol, prolactin = insulin antagonists → ↑ resistance, ↓ tissue sensitivity.
السؤال الكلاسيكي: "ليه الحمل بيسبب سكر؟" الإجابة: 5 هرمونات (hPL, E, P, Cortisol, Prolactin) بيشتغلوا ضد الإنسولين.
Risk factors
  • Family history of diabetes and hypertension
  • Obesity
  • Polyhydramnios
  • Mother > 45 years or < 20 years (as written in the book)
  • Multiparity
  • Repeated unexplained abortion or stillbirth
  • Previous macrosomic infant
الكتاب كاتب العمر "فوق 45 وتحت 20" – اكتبها زي الكتاب في الامتحان حتى لو شفتها في مصدر تاني 35.
Symptoms

Most of the time no noticeable symptoms. Possible: increased thirst, polyuria, dry mouth, tiredness, blurred eyesight, genital itching or thrush.

لو جالك سؤال "أغلب الحالات…" الإجابة: ما بتظهرش أعراض (asymptomatic).
Complications

Maternal: polyhydramnios, ↑ abortion, ↑ preeclampsia, ↑ perinatal mortality & morbidity, preterm labor, infection, APH & PPH, prolonged obstructed labor, puerperal sepsis.

Fetal / neonatal: fetal hyperglycemia or neonatal hypoglycemia, IUFD, congenital malformation, macrosomia, prematurity & RDS, neonatal hyperbilirubinemia.

Polyhydramnios بيظهر مرتين: risk factor وكمان maternal complication. ماتتلخبطش.

Diagnosis: GCT vs GTT

1) Glucose challenge test (1-hour, 50 g oral):

  • ≤ 139 mg/dl → negative, stop.
  • 140–184 → positive → needs 3-hour 100 g OGTT.
  • ≥ 185 → diagnostic, no further test.

2) GTT: done in the first part of the 3rd trimester. Fasting woman gets 100 g glucose orally; 4 samples (fasting, 1, 2, 3 h); two abnormal readings = positive.

Nursing assessment
  • Visits every 2 weeks until 28 wk, then weekly until 36 wk, then hospitalized till delivery.
  • Screening at the first prenatal visit for women with risk factors; observe signs and symptoms in all pregnant women.
  • Baseline vital signs, BP, fetal heart sounds – compare with later readings.
  • Serum AFP for early detection of congenital malformation.
  • HbA1c: glycemic control over previous 4–12 weeks; > 7 = elevated glucose.
  • Ultrasound at 18 weeks: confirm gestational age + detect congenital abnormalities.
  • Assess fetal wellbeing and psycho-socioeconomic support (social, emotional, economic).
Nursing intervention: nutrition
  • Cut down sweets; 3 small meals + 1–3 snacks; fixed meal times; fiber from fruits, vegetables, whole grains.
  • Normal-weight client: 2200–2500 kcal. Carbs ≥ 45% (200–300 g), fat 30% (70–80 g), protein 25% (100–125 g).
  • +30 g protein/day in 2nd and 3rd trimesters. Nephropathy + proteinuria → extra protein.
  • Distributed as 3 meals + 3 snacks, never less than 2000 kcal/day.
  • Complex carbs each meal (delay glucose absorption); adequate fat (delays gastric emptying, prevents hyperglycemia); evening snack of complex carb + protein prevents night hypoglycemia; avoid sweets.
الكتاب بيقول "3 meals + 1–3 snacks" في مكان و "3 meals + 3 snacks" في مكان تاني. لو الاختيارات فيها الاتنين، دور على الصياغة اللي في السؤال.
Insulin & blood glucose monitoring
  • Targets: < 95 on awakening, < 140 1 h after meal, < 120 2 h after meal.
  • Check 4 times/day: on rising, before lunch, before dinner, at bedtime. Keep a record and show the team.
  • Insulin is not harmful for the baby; dose individualized by clinical picture + glucose results.
  • 1st trimester 0.7, 2nd 0.8, 3rd 0.9–1.0 IU/kg. 2/3 in the morning, 1/3 in the evening. Usually mixture: long-acting + short-acting.
Exercise, delivery, breastfeeding
  • Pregnancy is not the time to start vigorous exercise. Exercise lowers blood glucose.
  • Well-controlled women who already exercise may continue; remind them to eat a carb or protein snack before activity.
  • Stop exercise if hypertensive complication signs appear.
  • Delivery: timing depends on complications; if none → term 37–40 wk. Vaginal if practicable; C/S if urgent delivery needed or malpresentation.
  • Diabetic mothers are encouraged to breastfeed.

Mnemonics for the hard parts

Anti-insulin hormones

hPL + E + P + C + P

“Help! Every Pregnant Chick’s Pancreas” – hPL, Estrogen, Progesterone, Cortisol, Prolactin.

Newborn problems

4H + RIBPT

4 H’s: Hypothermia, Hypoglycemia, Hypocalcemia, Hyperbilirubinemia. Then “RIBPT – Ribbit 🐸”: RDS, Infection, Bleeding, Polycythemia, Trauma.

Newborn care order

O-W-P-G-C-A-K

“Oh Wow, Please Give Calcium And K” – Oxygen, Warmth, Phototherapy, Glucose, Calcium, Antibiotics, vitamin K.

Glucose targets

95 – 140 – 120

Wake 95, then 1 h = 140, 2 h = 120. The 1 h number is always the biggest.

Insulin per trimester

7 · 8 · 9

0.7, 0.8, 0.9–1.0 climbing with the weeks. Then 2/3 morning, 1/3 evening (“big breakfast, small dinner”).

GCT cut-offs

139 · 140 · 185

139 = go home, 140 = go on to GTT, 185 = stop, it’s GDM.

Macronutrients

45 · 30 · 25

Carbs > Fat > Protein, in the order C-F-P: 200–300 / 70–80 / 100–125 g.

Visit schedule

2 – 28 | 1 – 36 | Hosp

Every 2 weeks until 28, every 1 week until 36, then hospital.

GTT readings

90 · 165 · 145 · 125

Starts at 90, jumps to 165, then steps down by 20: 145, 125.

Exam traps (spot them before they spot you)

"Positive GCT" معناها 140–184 مش 185. لو الرقم 185 أو أكتر يبقى diagnostic ومفيش test تاني.
GTT محتاج two abnormal readings مش واحدة.
Insulin "harmful for the baby"؟ غلط. الكتاب: Insulin is not harmful for the baby.
Hypoglycemia في الطفل سببها fetal hyperinsulinism. الـ prematurity سبب الـ RDS والـ hypothermia بس.
Hyperbilirubinemia سببها premature liver function مش hyperinsulinism.
Polycythemia سببها hypoxia of bone marrow (compensatory).
Exercise: ممنوع تبدأ vigorous exercise في الحمل، لكن اللي كانت بتمارس وهي controlled تكمل.
Snack قبل الرياضة = carbohydrate or protein. وsnack بالليل = complex carb + protein.
Mode of delivery: vaginal لو مفيش complications. C/S لو urgent أو malpresentation.
Breastfeeding: الأم السكرية تتشجع ترضّع، مش بتتمنع.
Vitamin K للـ bleeding، calcium للـ hypocalcemia، glucose للـ hypoglycemia – ماتبدلش.
Glucose target بعد الأكل بساعة < 140، بعد ساعتين < 120. ماتعكسهمش.
